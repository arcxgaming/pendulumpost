Pendulum Post — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026.

The short version

Pendulum Post collects no data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no use of location, contacts, camera or microphone. There is no server of ours: the app never sends anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress — deliveries completed, stamps, letters read, Postmarks, cosmetic choices and settings — is stored only on your iPhone. It is included in your device backup if you use one, and it is deleted when you delete the app. We cannot see it and cannot recover it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information from a purchase. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy. Restoring a purchase uses your Apple Account through Apple's own mechanism.

Links

The app can open this policy and the support page in your browser. Opening a web page is the only time the app hands anything to another app, and it hands over only the address of the page.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data, this policy and its effective date will change before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the studio.
