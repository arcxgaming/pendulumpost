Help

How to play

A brass-and-teal mail capsule swings from a pendulum. Tap the sky to release it. The capsule keeps the speed of the swing and flies on under gravity; reach the post box to deliver the letter. Each route also has three optional gold stamps to collect on the way. Nothing is timed and nothing is lost by waiting: the pendulum swings for as long as you like.

The guide

Dotted guidance shows where the capsule will go. The first deliveries show the whole path; later routes show only the start of the arc so the timing stays yours. You can turn the full path back on at any time under Settings → Comfort & accessibility → Full guidance always.

Panels, brambles and wind

Glass panels bounce the capsule; the numbers show the order the panels will be met. Brambles catch the capsule and end the flight gently; there is no penalty beyond trying again. Where a breeze blows, the screen says so and the dotted path already includes it.

A missed delivery

A miss costs nothing. Tap Try again and the swing starts over. If a route keeps catching you, Postmarks earned by playing can buy a bramble pass from the pause menu for that route.

Letters

Every first delivery brings a letter. They gather on the desk under Letters; tap an envelope to open it, and tap outside the paper to put it back. Unread letters keep their wax seal.

Lost progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone after every delivery and whenever the app goes to the background. It is included in your device backup. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Comfort settings

Settings has music, sound effects and haptics switches. Comfort & accessibility has the trajectory guide, full guidance, high-contrast flight, reduced motion and sharper rendering.

Purchases

Pendulum Post is free to begin. One one-time purchase, The Whole Journey, opens the rest of the lifetime story and all of Glass & Tide. There are no subscriptions, no consumable purchases and no advertising. Postmarks are earned by playing and cannot be bought.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Post office → Game & stories → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact

Write to the studio. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the route number if it is about a delivery. We usually reply within a few days.
